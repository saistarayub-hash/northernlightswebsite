04 — GOOD TO KNOWA LITTLE CLARITY

Your questions.
A little light.

Is this the official Northern Lights website?

Not yet. This is a design preview for Northern Lights Herbal Wellness in Lenasia. The business has not yet approved this website’s branding or content.

Where is Northern Lights located?

A public directory lists 142 Protea Avenue, Lenasia, Gauteng, 1821. The address is awaiting direct business confirmation. Check the current listing before travelling.

What are the opening hours?

Opening hours and holiday schedules have not been confirmed for this preview. We’ll add business-approved details rather than risk displaying outdated information.

Can I order or create an account here?

No. This is an informational dispensary website preview. It does not accept orders, payments, or customer account registrations. A separate staff editor manages opening hours, announcements, and FAQs.

Does this website collect personal information?

The public preview has no customer forms or tracking analytics. The connected staff backend stores staff accounts and edit history, with an essential sign-in cookie for authenticated staff. Google Fonts serves the typefaces and may receive standard request information, such as your IP address. The website host may also retain standard access logs. External websites have their own privacy policies.

Can I turn off the animation and sound?

Sound is off by default and can be toggled in the header. The site respects your device’s reduced-motion setting. You can also pause the visual effects using the button below.
