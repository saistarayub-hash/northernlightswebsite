Before you add to bag.

Can I actually buy these items?

Not yet. This is a working merchandise-store prototype with illustrative prices and stock. Demo orders do not charge money, reserve real products, or trigger fulfilment.

How do sizes, shipping, and returns work?

Sizes are demonstration options. Fit guides, manufacturing specifications, shipping services, and a returns policy must be approved before real checkout is enabled. Demo checkout has no delivery address or payment form.

What information does this demo store?

The shopping bag is stored in this browser. The connected demo backend stores your sample customer name, email, hashed password, and demo order history. Sign-in uses an essential session cookie. Please use fictional details and a password you do not use elsewhere.
