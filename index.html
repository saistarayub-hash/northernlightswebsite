07 — GOOD TO KNOW

A little
clarity.

Can I order or pay on this site?

No. This preview is a visual catalog — it does not take orders, payments, delivery requests or customer accounts. Stock and pricing are confirmed in store.

Are these the real prices and products?

Not yet. Products, brands and imagery are shown to demonstrate the finished catalog. Northern Lights confirms the final range, stock and pricing.

Is the 3D display interactive?

Yes. Drag to rotate the stage, or tap a product to bring it into focus. The display respects reduced-motion settings and falls back to an image if 3D is unavailable.

What are the opening hours?

Hours and holiday schedules have not yet been confirmed.
